Unboxing video required
Before opening, record one uninterrupted video that clearly shows the sealed parcel, shipping label, all outer sides, opening, and the print. Keep all packaging. This video is required to assess any damage claim.
Final sale
All art prints are non-returnable and non-refundable after delivery. Change of mind, ordering the wrong size, colour preference, screen-to-print variation, framing fit, or a delayed delivery does not qualify for a return or refund.
What counts as damage
Eligible damage means clear physical transit damage that materially affects the print: a tear, puncture, water staining, crushing, or a crease running through the artwork; or an incorrect item sent by the studio. Minor packaging marks that do not affect the print, normal print variation, damage after delivery, or a product altered after opening are not eligible.
Claim review
Report a claim within 48 hours of delivery with the continuous unboxing video, clear photographs, order number, and all original packaging. Claims without the required evidence cannot be assessed. Where the studio verifies eligible damage or an incorrect item, a fresh copy will be sent.

Artwork, photographs, writing, and design remain the artist’s intellectual property. They may not be copied, reproduced, redistributed, or used commercially without written permission.
